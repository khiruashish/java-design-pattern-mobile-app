# L3 · Professional

L3 🟡 Professional · Experience: 3–6 yrs

**Goal:** after this level you can apply patterns idiomatically in modern Java, frameworks and concurrent code.

**Who it's for:** engineers with roughly 3–6 yrs of Java experience, or anyone who finished the previous level.
